## Game Features and Functionality

### Football Simulation and Match Control

EA SPORTS FC 27 builds on the series' focus on simulated football, combining player movement, passing, shooting, defensive positioning, and tactical decision-making. Match outcomes depend on how players manage possession, create opportunities, and respond to the opposing team's formation.

Control options allow players to select a preferred approach to passing, shooting, switching defenders, and moving teammates. Learning these systems helps make attacking combinations more deliberate and defensive actions more consistent.

### Career Mode and Club Management

Career Mode focuses on the longer-term management and development of a football team or individual player. Manager-focused activities involve squad planning, transfers, tactical decisions, and objectives associated with running a club.

Player-focused activities emphasize individual development and a football career. The mode provides a different experience from single-match competition because decisions made between matches can influence team composition and future objectives.

### Football Ultimate Team

Football Ultimate Team centers on building a custom squad from available player items. Players can adjust formations, choose preferred players, improve selected cards through supported systems, and prepare a team for different match situations.

Squad building involves balancing positions, chemistry or team-link systems where applicable, and individual player attributes. Objectives, squad challenges, and seasonal activities provide additional ways to earn rewards and adjust a team's composition.

### The Grounds and Social Football

The Grounds introduces a social football playground designed to connect street-style activities with stadium football. It provides a distinct setting from traditional eleven-a-side matches and adds another way to experience the game's football environment.

The mode includes activities and social spaces that let players move between different types of football experiences. Its availability depends on the platform edition, so players should verify the supported features for their specific version.

### Clubs and Team-Based Competition

Clubs allows players to create or join a football club and participate in matches as part of a team. Instead of controlling an entire side in the same way as traditional match modes, participants can focus on their assigned player and coordinate with teammates.

Club identity, team formation, role selection, and communication can influence the experience. The mode is suited to players who prefer coordinated matches and a shared club identity rather than managing every position individually.

### Authentic Clubs, Players, and Competitions

EA SPORTS FC 27 features a broad catalogue of licensed football content. EA's published information describes more than 21,000 players, over 800 clubs and national teams, 140 stadiums, and more than 35 leagues.

The game includes major domestic competitions and international tournaments, with availability shaped by licensing agreements. Specific clubs, players, competitions, kits, and stadiums may differ by mode and content update.

### Tactical Formations and Squad Selection

Team selection and tactical configuration are central to match preparation. Formations determine how players occupy the pitch, while tactical instructions influence defensive shape, attacking width, pressing behaviour, and movement during possession.

Squad choices can be adapted to different match objectives. Players may favour possession, rapid transitions, wide attacks, or a more defensive structure depending on the available personnel and preferred playing style.

### Player Attributes and Team Chemistry

Player attributes affect how individuals perform in different situations. Pace, passing, shooting, defensive ability, physical characteristics, and other ratings can influence which players are suitable for a particular position or tactical approach.

In squad-building modes, team composition can also involve compatibility systems and role considerations. Understanding how players complement one another helps create a more balanced side than selecting players based on individual ratings alone.

### Match Presentation and Stadium Atmosphere

Stadium environments, crowd reactions, commentary, player animations, and broadcast-style presentation contribute to the matchday atmosphere. Presentation options and camera settings allow players to tailor the visual experience to their preferences.

The combination of licensed venues and football presentation helps distinguish competitions and clubs. Available stadiums and presentation elements depend on the content included in the game.

### Controls, Settings, and Accessibility

The PC edition provides settings for graphics, display, audio, and input configuration. Players can adjust available controls and camera options to suit their equipment and preferred way of playing.

Controller and keyboard configurations can offer different approaches to movement and match control. Reviewing the settings before beginning a competitive session can help establish consistent controls and a comfortable visual layout.

### Seasonal Content and In-Game Objectives

The game includes activities and objectives associated with its supported modes. These can provide additional goals beyond completing individual matches, including squad challenges, club activities, and seasonal rewards.

The availability and requirements of particular objectives can change as the game receives content updates. Check the current in-game menus and official announcements for the details of active activities and their conditions.
